Who is responsible

Merlin Schwabe, operating Explainory. Contact: explainoryhq@gmail.com.

What the Explainory app is

The Explainory app is a private publishing tool that runs on the operator's own computer. It uploads Explainory's own videos to Explainory's own accounts on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram. It is not offered to the public, and no other person can sign in to it.

Data the app processes

When the Explainory account is connected via the official login of a platform (for example TikTok Login Kit), the app receives:

  • access and refresh tokens and the account ID (open_id) of the connected Explainory account,
  • basic profile and creator information of that account (name, avatar, allowed privacy settings, maximum video length),
  • the status of uploads made by the app.

The app does not collect data about viewers, followers or any other users, and it does not use cookies or analytics on this website.

Purpose and legal basis

The data is used only to publish and manage Explainory's own videos (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored encrypted on the operator's computer and are never sold or shared with third parties. Videos and their captions are sent only to the platform they are published on. Data is deleted when the connection is revoked or no longer needed.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions).

Your rights
